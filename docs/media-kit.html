Licensed to Kill (LTK)

The community for the whole pest control trade. Marcus Scruggs started LTK on 1 January 2025 so pest pros could get to know each other. What began as a video game group is now a full hub: the Discord, a podcast, tournaments and the LTK Community Hub website.

Who’s here

  • Pest control technicians, owners and specialists from across the US
  • All corners of the trade: general pest, termite, wildlife, exclusion and every field in between
  • Gamers: tournaments, game nights and a fantasy football league
  • An open public invite — no licence check to join

Where we show up

  • The LTK Discord — where the community lives day to day
  • YouTube — the podcast, industry interviews and tournament streams
  • The LTK Facebook group and LinkedIn
  • www.ltkpmp.com — field guides, state licensing, ACE prep and the Arena
  • In person — meetups and trade shows, including PestWorld
