**The lever:** +2.13 points in 90 days comes almost entirely from one decision — server-render the
property on an owned domain with a proper hub skeleton and a full `@graph`. Six dimensions move at
once. No amount of copywriting or design polish moves a single point until that lands.

Re-score at day 90, 180 and 365 against the same nine-property benchmark. Score movement per
dimension is the cleanest progress narrative to put in front of a sponsor or an investor, and it
is the same instrument used across the rest of the portfolio, so the numbers compare.
